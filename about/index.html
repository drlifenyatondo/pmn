About Purity Mufarowashe Nyatondo

Software Engineer, Founder & Tech Specialist at TumaiLife Technologies Co., Zvandiri Peer Supporter, and IAS Change Maker, U-Report Champion.

// MY JOURNEY

Dedicated to Digital Innovation & Community Empowerment

Based in Chitungwiza and Harare, Zimbabwe, I work at the intersection of full-stack web engineering and global health advocacy.

As the Founder and Tech Specialist at TumaiLife Technologies Co., I design dynamic web applications, offline-first survey systems, and custom database backends built for real-world reliability.

Through my role as a Peer Supporter with Zvandiri and champion under the READY Movement (READY+), I lead peer counselling, HIV awareness campaigns, and mental health workshops for young people.
