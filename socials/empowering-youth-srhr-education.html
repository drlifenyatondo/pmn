Empowering Youth through Comprehensive SRHR Education

Purity M. Nyatondo 6 min read SRHR Rights

Sexual and Reproductive Health Rights (SRHR) are fundamental to adolescent autonomy, health outcomes, and gender equality. Comprehensive education equips young people with the information necessary to make informed health choices.

Regional Insights: READY Academy

Through initiatives like the READY Academy under the READY Movement, youth advocates collaborate across borders to lobby for youth-friendly health services, policy reforms, and bodily autonomy.

Key Objectives

  • Barrier Removal: Eliminating age restrictions and legal hurdles accessing healthcare.
  • Policy Inclusion: Ensuring youth representatives actively shape regional health budgets.
  • Community Awareness: Dispelling misconceptions surrounding reproductive health.
