The Power of Peer-Led HIV Care Systems

Purity M. Nyatondo 5 min read HIV Advocacy

Adolescents and young people living with HIV often navigate unique psychosocial barriers, including stigma, disclosure challenges, and treatment fatigue. Peer-led healthcare delivery models bridge the gap between clinical care and community reality.

The Zvandiri Model

Organizations like Zvandiri demonstrate that trained peer supporters—young people living with HIV themselves—are uniquely positioned to foster trust, encourage antiretroviral therapy (ART) adherence, and provide empathetic counseling.

Core Pillars of Impact

  • Improved Adherence: Shared lived experience motivates consistent medication intake and clinic visits.
  • Stigma Reduction: Open peer dialogue normalizes living with HIV and dismantles internal stigma.
  • Holistic Support: Connecting clinical health outcomes with mental health care and social empowerment.
