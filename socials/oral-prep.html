Oral PrEP: Daily & Event-Driven HIV Protection

Purity M. Nyatondo 4 min read HIV Prevention

Oral PrEP is a safe, proven pill taken by HIV-negative people to prevent getting HIV. When taken consistently as prescribed, Oral PrEP reduces the risk of getting HIV from sex by about 99%.

Ways to Take Oral PrEP

  • Daily Oral PrEP: Taking one pill every day at roughly the same time. Suitable for everyone, including cisgender women, cisgender men, and transgender individuals.
  • Event-Driven PrEP (2-1-1): Taking 2 pills 2–24 hours before sex, 1 pill 24 hours after the first dose, and 1 pill 24 hours later. Recommended specifically for people assigned male at birth who are not taking estrogen-based hormones.

Starting Safely & Side Effects

You need an HIV-negative test before starting Oral PrEP and regular HIV check-ups every three months. Mild side effects like slight nausea or headaches can occur during the first few days, but they usually clear up quickly as your body adjusts.
